# Week 21 - Capstone scope and evaluation plan

**Status:** teaching draft, not learner-tested. Fictional data and worked examples are labelled throughout. No production service is deployed by completing this lesson.

**Time:** 6 hours (360 learning minutes), with breaks additional. **Prerequisites:** the earlier course phases; review unfamiliar terms with the instructor.

**Materials:** this guide, the supplied workbook and diagram, a document editor or paper, and optionally an approved AI assistant. Do not buy a subscription for a lab without checking the dated cohort access plan.

## Learning outcomes

- Justify a feasible capstone and baseline.
- Separate development from held-out evaluation.
- Choose measurable criteria and mandatory release gates.

## Start with a prediction

Before reading the worked solution, write what you expect to happen, what could be wrong, and what evidence you would need to trust the result. Keep the note for your final reflection.

## Learner explanations

### Independent work is explainable work

You can extend the studio project, but must explain the additional scope and the decisions you own. Reusing an artifact is acceptable when disclosed. Copying an impressive demonstration without understanding the evidence does not establish professional competence.

### Keep some evidence unused

Prepare at least 12 distinct final evaluation cases including at least three difficult or unsupported cases. At least four remain unused during initial development. For projects requiring 20 cases, such as the voice or knowledge routes, use the larger requirement and keep an unused subset.

### Predeclare the gates

Specify the expected result and scoring rule for each case before evaluating. Separate ordinary quality thresholds from data, rights, authorisation and safety blockers. Identify who can approve a release and how to continue learning if real deployment is not authorised.

## Worked example

A 12-case plan can keep two unsupported and two recovery cases sealed while developing on the other eight. If the chosen project requires 20 cases, expand the matrix rather than replacing its requirement with the course minimum. A denied approval case passes when no send occurs.

## Guided lab

1. Choose the capstone scope and disclose reused studio material.
2. Write user, baseline, acceptance criteria and exclusions.
3. Create the evaluation matrix with expected outputs before building.
4. Mark at least four cases held out and arrange instructor custody where feasible.
5. Define required approvals, budget, owners and release gates.
6. Submit for scope review; revise before significant implementation.

## Professional transfer

Choose your own professional context and write one safe adaptation: an engineering document review, a public health-education task, a campaign brief, or an art/design workflow. Name the authorised reviewer, permitted inputs and one task outside scope. Use invented data unless you have explicit permission; do not make a second full project this week.

## Assessment

| Dimension | Maximum points |
| --- | --- |
| Bound an independent contribution | 4 |
| Declare useful criteria | 4 |
| Separate evaluation data | 4 |
| Identify real release gates | 4 |

Provisional pass: **12/16** with no unresolved safety-critical issue. Score each dimension 0 (missing/unsafe), 1 (major gaps), 2 (partial), 3 (sound with minor omissions), or 4 (clear and evidenced). A learner may pass by honestly diagnosing a failed workflow; this is not production authorisation.

## Safety and required remediation

Do not prefill a production approval as granted. Hold the release until the actual owner and required professional reviewers decide.

Correct any unauthorised data use, fabricated evidence, hidden failure, unapproved consequential action or false claim of production testing before passing. Keep the actual release state explicit.

## Instructor companion and answer guidance

**Expected worked result:** A 12-case plan can keep two unsupported and two recovery cases sealed while developing on the other eight. If the chosen project requires 20 cases, expand the matrix rather than replacing its requirement with the course minimum. A denied approval case passes when no send occurs.

**1. Can you reuse studio work?**

Yes, with disclosure and a justified independent extension.

**2. Which requirement wins: 12 generic cases or a 20-case project minimum?**

The larger applicable project requirement.

**3. Can an unsupported request pass by being declined?**

Yes, if that is the declared correct behaviour.

**Facilitation:** ask the learner to point to a source, calculation, state transition or artifact supporting their conclusion. Accept defensible alternatives with explicit assumptions. Do not reward confident wording without evidence.

**Remediation:** identify the failed criterion, explain its consequence, provide a new small input, and reassess the relevant behaviour. Record what was actually observed; do not issue professional approval from a course score.
