**Status:** teaching draft, not learner-tested. Fictional data and worked examples are labelled throughout. No production service is deployed by completing this lesson.

## Learner explanations

### Different audio tasks

Speech recognition converts speech to text. Text-to-speech synthesises audio from text. Voice cloning attempts to reproduce characteristics of a voice and requires the appropriate permissions and service rules. A synthetic voice does not remove responsibility for the script's claims.

### A sequence must hold together

Video quality includes continuity, timing, legibility, sound and the relationship between narration and image. Generate or edit short segments against a storyboard, then check the assembled sequence. Keep the project files, script versions and source references, not only the final export.

### Accessibility and consent

Check names, numbers, synchronisation and meaning in captions; do not assume automatic transcription is correct. Obtain voice/likeness permission and recording consent where applicable. Commercial use may require a qualifying plan and excludes some beta services; verify the exact current terms before publishing.

## Glossary

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Transcription | Turning speech into a text record. |
| Speech synthesis | Generating speech from text or another permitted input. |
| Storyboard | A sequence planning the content and purpose of shots. |
| Caption | A checked text representation of relevant speech and sounds. |

## Guided lab

1. Write a 12-30 second approved script and three-shot storyboard.
2. Record asset sources, intended usage and any required consent.
3. Generate or record permitted narration; use ElevenLabs only with suitable rights.
4. Assemble visuals and captions in an approved editor if available.
5. Audit script, audio, captions and visuals separately, then the combined sequence.
6. Use the offline timecard/caption correction route if media tools are unavailable and label unassessed audio outcomes.

Provisional pass: **12/16** with no unresolved safety-critical issue. Score each dimension 0 (missing/unsafe), 1 (major gaps), 2 (partial), 3 (sound with minor omissions), or 4 (clear and evidenced). A learner may pass by honestly diagnosing a failed workflow; this is not production authorisation.

## Safety and required remediation

Do not clone an identifiable person's voice without proper consent and verification. Do not label an educational plan as a produced or commercially cleared video.

Correct any unauthorised data use, fabricated evidence, hidden failure, unapproved consequential action or false claim of production testing before passing. Keep the actual release state explicit.
