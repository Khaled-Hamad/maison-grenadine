# Considered Practice

The visual language is a study in clear decisions. Generous white space separates one idea from the next; deep navy gives structure to the journey. The work should feel meticulously crafted, with alignment and proportion doing more than decoration. Complexity belongs in the subject, not in the interface.

Gold is a measured accent, never a field of competing signals. On dark surfaces it marks a beginning or a consequential number; on light surfaces, deep gold carries readable emphasis. White, Mist and Champagne leave room for observation. Colour never replaces a status label, a source, or a written instruction.

The supplied logo is an image, not a typographic approximation. Its aspect ratio and clear space remain intact. Segoe UI supplies a familiar, calm reading rhythm; Semibold identifies the next decision. Master-level execution here means consistent hierarchy, short line lengths and charts that can be understood without ornament.

Progress is expressed through evidence: a marked source, a reviewed draft, a documented test, a handover accepted by another person. Diagrams turn those relationships into spatial form. Their boxes describe real responsibilities, and their arrows name what moves. Painstaking attention is given to the boundary between a proposal and an authorised action.

Every surface should remain useful without animation, a subscription or a perfect network connection. The craftsmanship is most visible in what has been removed: misleading completion badges, decorative jargon, inaccessible gold-on-white text and promises the material cannot support. The resulting identity is practical, warm and deliberately restrained.

## Applied brand reference

Derived from the user-supplied Khaled Hamad Brand Guidelines v3.2 and Slide Master Template.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#182234` | Primary text and panels |
| Deep Navy | `#0F1626` | Dark surfaces and footers |
| Mid Navy | `#26324D` | Secondary dark panels |
| Gold | `#CAAD6A` | Accent on navy; non-text rules on light |
| Deep Gold | `#806226` | Accent text on light |
| Steel | `#3D4A63` | Secondary text |
| Slate | `#5E6A82` | Captions on light |
| Silver Blue | `#B9C2D3` | Secondary text on navy |
| Mist | `#F3F5F8` | Light panels |
| Champagne | `#F6EFDD` | Review callouts |
| Line | `#DDE2EA` | Light borders |
| Type | Segoe UI / Segoe UI Semibold | Brand-specified; Arial fallback |

Use British spelling. Keep slide titles within 34-40 pt where practical, body text 16-18 pt and captions at least 12 pt. Use navy logo artwork on light and gold artwork on navy, with no stretching, recolouring, shadows or retyped wordmark. Website logo images retain the supplied proportions.

Original brand archives, original Office templates, contact templates and personal correspondence are not included in the public curriculum source or download bundles.
